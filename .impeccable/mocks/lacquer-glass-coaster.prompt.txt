Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile web app composition comp, option C for LatteArt
Primary request: Design a complete production-grade portrait mobile interface for an interactive latte art simulator, preserving the chosen mature retro café lacquer-service-tray world while making a circular liquid-glass coaster the organizing structure. This is a third composition of the same visual direction, not a new brand.
Surface structure: edge-to-edge near-black oxblood lacquer ground; a compact quiet header with “LatteArt” at left and “繁中 / EN” plus one settings icon at right; below, one monumental circular clear-glass coaster occupying most of the viewport width. A matte porcelain coffee cup/canvas sits concentrically inside the upper portion of this coaster, visibly showing editable milk-and-coffee flow with a single subtle interaction trace. The coaster’s thick refractive rim extends beneath the cup and forms three large curved tool lenses around its lower arc, all in easy thumb reach. A small Reset control sits on the central axis below the arc. A narrow collapsed advanced-settings control is integrated into the bottom edge.
Signature composition: cup, coaster, and controls read as one concentric service object. The three lower-arc lenses are physically part of the glass coaster rather than separate cards. They are labeled exactly “Pour”, “Stir”, “Lift” and use restrained authored copper line icons. Pour is selected through a wine-red glass fill, a raised rim, and a small copper marker. The refractive glass bends the lacquer texture and creates gentle edge caustics without obscuring labels.
Style/medium: polished shippable mobile product UI mockup, crisp semantic controls, refined retro café ritual with optically thick liquid-glass material; not futuristic OS chrome.
Color palette: near-black oxblood #2B1718, wine #6F2430, parchment #F2E9D8, brushed copper #C78455, porcelain #FBFAF4, natural coffee brown. Glass is clear smoked crystal with a slight oxblood tint.
Materials/textures: authentic dark hand lacquer, matte porcelain, thick hand-blown liquid glass, sparse brushed copper inlays. Controlled highlights; labels remain highly legible.
Composition/framing: full-bleed 390×844-style portrait app screen, no device frame; compact header, dominant concentric cup/coaster, radial controls kept entirely inside the lower thumb zone; no scrolling required.
Typography: mature refined humanist sans serif, concise and fully legible.
Text (verbatim, each once): “LatteArt”, “繁中 / EN”, “Pour”, “Stir”, “Lift”, “Reset”.
Constraints: unmistakably an operable latte art simulator; exactly three primary tools immediately available; advanced settings collapsed; touch targets at least 44px; selected state does not rely on color alone; no marketing copy; no extra words; no emoji; use authored line icons; no childish colors; no phone frame; no hands; no café photograph; no watermark; no bento cards; no generic floating pill stack; no neon; no frosted-white glass overlay; no illegible microcopy. Liquid glass must behave as a functional café serving object native to the lacquer world.
