Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile web app composition comp, option B for LatteArt
Primary request: Design a complete production-grade portrait mobile interface for an interactive latte art simulator, preserving the mature retro café lacquer-service-tray world while introducing a distinctive liquid-glass serving rail. This is a second composition of an already chosen visual direction, not a new brand.
Surface structure in order: an edge-to-edge deep oxblood hand-lacquer ground; a compact header with “LatteArt” left and “繁中 / EN” plus one settings icon right; a very large circular matte-porcelain latte canvas occupying roughly the top two-thirds, visibly showing editable milk-and-coffee flow with one restrained touch trace; a single continuous floating refractive glass tool rail overlapping the lower edge of the cup; a small subordinate Reset control beneath; a thin collapsed advanced-settings glass lip anchored at the bottom.
Signature composition: the three primary tools live inside one horizontally stretched, organically curved transparent glass vessel—like molten clear glass laid across the lacquer tray. Its edge bends and refracts the dark wine surface underneath, with subtle caustic highlights and copper catches. The rail slightly overlaps the cup to tighten the thumb-reach layout. Divide the vessel into exactly three generous tool zones labeled “Pour”, “Stir”, “Lift”; Pour is selected with a deep translucent wine fill plus a clear underline, not color alone.
Style/medium: polished shippable mobile product UI mockup, crisp semantic controls, realistic refraction used only on the header chips, tool rail, and collapsed drawer; tactile café ritual, not futuristic operating system UI.
Color palette: near-black oxblood #2B1718, wine #6F2430, parchment #F2E9D8, brushed copper #C78455, porcelain #FBFAF4, natural coffee brown. Glass is clear smoke with a faint wine tint.
Materials/textures: authentic hand lacquer texture, matte porcelain, brushed copper hairlines, optically thick liquid glass with readable refraction and controlled highlights.
Composition/framing: full-bleed 390×844-style portrait app screen, no device frame, compact header, enormous cup, overlapping glass rail in lower-thumb reach; all controls fit one viewport.
Typography: mature refined humanist sans serif, concise and fully legible.
Text (verbatim, each once): “LatteArt”, “繁中 / EN”, “Pour”, “Stir”, “Lift”, “Reset”.
Constraints: unmistakably an operable latte art simulator; no marketing copy; no extra words; advanced settings visibly collapsed; touch targets at least 44px; selected state uses shape and underline; high contrast; no emoji; use authored line icons for pitcher, stirring gesture, lifted drop, reset, settings; no childish colors; no phone frame; no hands; no café photograph; no watermark; no generic card grid; no excessive pills; no frosted white glass sheets; no neon; no illegible microcopy. The liquid glass must feel like a functional café serving object integrated with the lacquer direction, not a detached Apple-style effect.
